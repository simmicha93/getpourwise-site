Good to know

A few useful
details.

Clear inputs make useful costing. Here’s what to expect before we start.

What do you need from my bar?

Your current drink list, exact bottle brands and sizes, purchase costs (invoices or current supplier pricing), and any house specs you already use. For a clean handoff, include recipes for drinks you want to preserve.

Are the cost and price numbers guaranteed?

No. Supplier pricing, yields, waste, tax, and local menu prices change. The workbook shows source dates and flags unverified inputs. Its suggested price is a planning calculation from supplied costs and your chosen pour-cost target—not a sales or profit guarantee.

Why are some sample inputs marked unverified?

Because an unverified price should not be presented as fact. In the sample, the espresso-shot cost is left blank and affected pricing stays provisional until a real cost and measured yield are supplied.

Does the $497 program include batch recipes?

Yes. The Full Program includes the costed menu, batch recipes with yield notes, and a concise staff spec sheet. Batch yields are tied to your service assumptions and should be checked in your bar before rollout.

How do I pay?

Click any buy button to check out securely through Stripe. Card details are handled by Stripe and never touch this site.

Do you work outside Panama City Beach?

The offer is designed for independent bars in Panama City and Panama City Beach, Florida. Confirm delivery format and availability with the service owner before purchase.
